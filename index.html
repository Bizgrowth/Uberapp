Ride Rules
When to accept, decline, and protect your metrics
✅
Always Accept
✓
Rides showing $8+ upfront fare
Even short rides in surge zones stack fast. Take it.
✓
Any ride during surge 1.5x or higher

✓
JAX Airport pickups
10–20 min drives, $15–25 base fares. Consistent quality rides.
✓
Rides during first 30 days of building rating
Accept everything until you have 200+ rated trips. Rating is money.
⚠️
Consider Declining
—
Pickup 10+ min away, small fare showing
Use Trip Radar (no acceptance rate hit) to check for better nearby rides first.
—
Rides toward rural dead zones
Callahan, Bryceville, Baldwin — hard return. Only take if fare $20+.
—
Uber Eats orders under $2/mile ratio
Quick math: Payout ÷ total miles. Under $2/mile? Skip it.
🛑
Always Decline — Safety First
✕
Any pickup that feels wrong
Trust your gut immediately. Cancel within 2 min of accepting = no cancellation rate hit.
✕
Rider rated under 4.5 stars
Low-rated riders = higher incident risk. You get to see their rating before accepting.
👩 Female driver tip: North side late-night pickups after 1am carry higher risk. Position in Southside, San Marco, and Beach zones instead after midnight.
📊
Uber Pro Metrics — Protect These
Acceptance Rate Keep 70%+
Gold status requires ~70%. Trip Radar declines DON'T count against you — use it to cherry-pick without penalty.
Star Rating Keep 4.85+
Below 4.6 risks deactivation. One bad rating is diluted by 10 five-stars. Prioritize the experience every ride.
Cancellation Rate Keep under 5%
Cancel BEFORE accepting = no hit. Cancel after = counts. Rider cancels = no hit to you.
